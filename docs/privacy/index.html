Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026 · App: Gaurav Jyotish by AuthorGaurav (Android, package com.gauravmishra.jyotish)

In short: the birth details you enter stay on your phone. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing about you is sent anywhere unless you choose to send it yourself.

Who we are

The app is made by Gaurav Mishra (AuthorGaurav), based in Singapore. Contact: gaurav.mishra65@gmail.com · authorgaurav.com.

What the app keeps on your phone

  • Birth details you add for yourself, family or clients: name, gender, date, time and place of birth, the place where the person lives now (optional) and a group label (optional).
  • Calculated charts, forecasts and reports made from those details.
  • Your settings, such as language and chart style.
  • Optional registration: your name, email and phone, if you enter them.
  • Unlock details (demo edition): the email and unlock code you enter to open the full version.

All of this is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. All calculations are done on the phone. The app does not upload this data, and it is excluded from Android cloud backup and from phone-to-phone transfer.

What leaves your phone, and only when you choose

  • Registration email. After you register, the app offers to open your own email app with a message to Gaurav Mishra containing your name, email, phone, language, app version and date. It is sent only if you tap Send in your email app. Birth details are never included.
  • Unlock code request. If you ask for an unlock code, your email app opens with a similar message (name, email, phone, app version). Again, it is sent only if you tap Send.
  • Sharing a report. If you tap Share or Save as PDF on a report, it goes only to the app or printer you choose.
  • Website and email links on the About screen open your browser or email app.

Emails you send us are used only to reply to you, to send your unlock code, and to keep a record of who is using the app. They are never sold or shared with anyone else.

Payments

  • Google Play edition: the full version is bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment and keeps the purchase record under the Google Privacy Policy. The app only checks with Google Play whether the full version has been bought; it never sees your card or bank details.
  • Demo edition (unlock code): if you pay by a payment link (for example Razorpay, Stripe, PayPal or UPI), that provider processes the payment under its own privacy policy. We receive the payment confirmation and your email, which we use to make your unlock code.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files. The demo and full editions ask for no permissions at all. The Google Play edition uses Google Play Billing only for the purchase.

Google Play may collect basic crash and performance data for apps it distributes (Android vitals), according to your device settings and Google's policies.

Children

The app is intended for adults. Parents may add a child's birth chart; it stays on the parent's phone like any other profile. We do not knowingly collect information from children. The app never makes predictions about marriage for anyone under 18.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit or delete any profile, and clear your registration, inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.
  • To see, correct or delete anything you have emailed us, write to gaurav.mishra65@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days.

About the readings

Readings describe what astrological factors are traditionally associated with. They are not predictions or guarantees, and they are not medical, legal or financial advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
